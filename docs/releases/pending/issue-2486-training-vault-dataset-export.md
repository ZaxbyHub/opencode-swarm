Adds the consented training vault and governed dataset export (issue #2486, Workstream D PR 7).

- Training-content capture is OFF by default and cannot be enabled by config, environment variables, prompts, agents, or tools. The only enabler is a durable, project-bound consent record under `.swarm/training/v1/consent.json`, granted through the human-only `/swarm dataset consent` two-step flow (terms preview + 15-minute confirm token); quotas clamp to hard ceilings (1 GiB / 250k records / 30 days — lower allowed, higher never). A consent copied to another project root is inert (project-binding digest fails closed).
- Capture is wired through the real hook chain (a read-only messages-transform step plus a fail-open tool-after step consuming the shared callID args snapshot), records lineage/role/labels/provenance/redaction-state/consent lineage per record, skips plugin-injected guidance carriers by construction, dedups repeat observations, and re-checks consent on every observation — revoke or expiry stops capture mid-stream while already-captured records keep their original consent lineage.
- The vault append path stops with a typed reason at quota/disk floors (never evicts unexpired records; tombstones are never deleted), quarantines corrupt lines with typed reasons, and reports health via the new counts-only `training_vault_health` telemetry event.
- `/swarm dataset export` (human-only, preview + confirm token) writes a deterministic bundle — records.jsonl, train.jsonl, validation.jsonl, and a manifest with sha256 checksums, echoed filters, and a session-coherent train/validation split — under `.swarm/training/v1/exports/<export-id>/`; identical re-exports are idempotent. `/swarm dataset withdraw` (human-only, preview + confirm token) physically empties the vault, appends a durable withdrawal tombstone, and writes REVOKED.json revocation manifests into every export still under plugin control.
- The retention registry's `planned-training-vault` placeholder is replaced by implemented `training-vault` and `training-exports` rows; `/swarm export` (plan+context JSON) is unchanged.
